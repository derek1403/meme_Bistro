# Why did you become a pilot?——To overcome my biggest fear: dying alone

![Why did you become a pilot?——To overcome my biggest fear: dying alone](../images/m2328-pilot-fear-dying-alone.png)

**一句話**：「你為什麼當機師？」「為了克服我最大的恐懼……」「懼高？」機長回頭微笑：「孤獨地死去。」——要死也要帶一整架乘客。
