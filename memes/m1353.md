# Car + Pet = Carpet

![Car + Pet = Carpet](../images/m1353-car-pet-carpet.jpg)

**一句話**：Car（汽車）——一台車；Pet（寵物）——一隻狗；Carpet（地毯）——被車輾扁、濺滿血的狗。
