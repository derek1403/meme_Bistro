# U good bro? No bro I wish that was me

![U good bro? No bro I wish that was me](../images/m3704-u-good-bro-wish-that-was-me.png)

**一句話**：動畫裡男女主角擁抱、依偎、甜蜜互動的四格；下面一隻貓痛苦地倒在地上：「U good bro?」「No bro I wish that was me.」
