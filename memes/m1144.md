# Nothing really mattress, who chairs

![Nothing really mattress, who chairs](../images/m1144-nothing-really-mattress.png)

**一句話**：路邊丟棄的床墊噴著「nothing really mattress」，椅子噴著「who chairs」。

## 🍸 笑點解說
- **梗在哪**：mattress ≈ matters、chairs ≈ cares——「沒什麼真的重要，誰在乎」。
