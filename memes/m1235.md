# Never argue with left-handed people

![Never argue with left-handed people](../images/m1235-left-handed-not-right.png)

**一句話**：Never argue with left-handed people. They are not Right.

## 🍸 笑點解說
- **梗在哪**：Right 同時是「右（手）」和「對的」——左撇子不是右撇子，所以「他們不對」。
