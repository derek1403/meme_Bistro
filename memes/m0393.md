# I don't care about your π-nion

![I don't care about your π-nion](../images/m0393-dont-care-about-your-pi-nion.png)

**一句話**：「別再講代數笑話了，我們都聽不懂。」「I don't care about your（π＋洋蔥）。」

## 🍸 笑點解說
- **梗在哪**：π（pi）＋ onion（洋蔥）＝ opinion（意見）。
