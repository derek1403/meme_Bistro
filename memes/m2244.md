# Don't fart in an Apple Store——They don't have Windows

![Don't fart in an Apple Store——They don't have Windows](../images/m2244-dont-fart-apple-store.png)

**一句話**：Windows 美國隊長：「別在 Apple Store 放屁。」Apple 光頭：「為什麼？」「因為他們沒有 windows（窗戶）。」——然後被整個電梯的人圍毆。
